A single-page player-reference and image-prompt guide for the *Cyberpunk RED* tabletop RPG,
compiled from publicly available rules summaries and community sources.

**Fan-work disclaimer.** *Cyberpunk RED* is the property of R. Talsorian Games; *Cyberpunk* is
also associated with CD Projekt. This is an unaffiliated, non-commercial fan reference; it is
not endorsed by or affiliated with either company, and reproduces no rulebook text beyond
short summaries for table use.
