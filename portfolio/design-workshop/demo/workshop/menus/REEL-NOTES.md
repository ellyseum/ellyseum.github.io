# The five title-flow treatments — reel notes (2026-07-26)

Reel: `out/letsplay/TITLE-FLOW-REEL.mp4` (7m28s; each treatment behind a house-register
title card). Individual clips: `out/letsplay/<slug>_r*.mp4`. Every clip is the SAME driven
path — attract → menu-in → hover walk → commit → save-select → naming → exposition — so
differences are treatment, not performance.

## What separated them (my read; the critic's comparative pass is the second opinion)

- **COLD LEDGER** — the ink-written title and the still, all-at-once menu genuinely deliver
  restraint-as-gravity. Quietest, most confident, most "the ledger is already open."
- **STAMP & STRIKE** — hardest identity: stamped title, snapping menu, the vermillion
  streak-cut commit. The clearest commit-grammar of the five.
- **THE SLOW BURN (r2)** — the warm grade now reads (r1's did not); the crack-into-black
  confirm is the most dramatic handoff, and its embers give the idle the most life.
- **CASINO FANFARE** — the dealt menu and coin-fountain commit are the most playful;
  furthest from the administrative register, closest to the Balatro juice pole.
- **THE ARCHIVE** — the shaft-lit title and center-out menu are the most atmospheric; the
  drip-line interrupt is the only treatment whose idle SAYS anything.

## Standing observations across all five (shared work, not treatment-specific)

1. The **save-select screen** is the flattest beat in every clip — the crystals read as dark
   diamonds at grading exposure; it wants its own lookdev pass.
2. The **exposition scrawl** holds on near-black for a long beat in all five; a treatment-
   colored backdrop (the burn's parchment, the archive's shaft) would carry the register
   through instead of dropping it.
3. The vista's **dusk palette is treatment-neutral** — only the grade wash differentiates
   the world. A per-treatment sky (or time-of-day) would deepen the identities.
